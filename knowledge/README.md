# Knowledge primers: the research-discussion round

The coding labs teach your hands. These teach what you must be able to SAY: how the
models in a VLA + world-model stack work, why they are designed that way, and where they
break. Each primer is the interview layer; the book in `reference/journal/book/` is the
depth layer (per-paper chapters, your own gate results).

## How to study a primer (30–45 min, on 2h days or weekends)

1. Read the "Mechanism" section once, slowly. Draw the diagram yourself on paper.
2. Close the file. Write the key equation(s) from memory.
3. Answer the questions OUT LOUD before opening the `<details>` answer. Mark each:
   ✅ said it cleanly · 🟡 got there slowly · ❌ did not know.
4. Re-ask yourself only the 🟡 and ❌ questions three days later.

The answers are deliberately short. In an interview, a precise 60-second answer followed
by "I can go deeper on X" beats a 5-minute tour.
