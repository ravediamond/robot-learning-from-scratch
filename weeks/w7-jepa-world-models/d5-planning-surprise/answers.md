# Model answers

1. Typical: random ~0.02, latent-L2 ~0.3–0.45, probe-decoded ~0.8. Same predictor, same CEM: the dynamics are good enough to plan; the latent L2 distance is the weak part (it does not measure progress in the world).
2. ~0.25–0.4: JEPA only asks the predictor to match the next embedding and SIGReg to keep the distribution Gaussian; nothing asks distant states to be distant in latent: the encoder can fold space. Fixes: plan with a learned cost (a progress/reward head, or a probe/decoder for the task variable), add a temporal-distance objective (latent distance ∝ steps between frames: your KB's Temporal-Distance-JEPA / "LeWM as reward"), goal-conditioned value functions, or plan over short horizons with subgoals.
3. Large teleports are trivially detected; small ones (0.1) much less: they look like normal motion. Real failures (a slip of a few mm, a bowl tilting) are small, gradual and often look like normal dynamics early on: surprise is a weak monitor for them, especially when the model never saw failures. Compare against the length null.
4. H0 (reranking candidate chunks by predicted outcome) = planner-like use (choosing actions by imagined results): failed because candidates barely differed, not even because of the model. H3 (predict failure from real rollouts) = evaluator role. Today's toy: dynamics can be good while the cost/metric is the bottleneck: for H0/H3, check the scoring signal (what the latent distance or surprise actually measures) before blaming the dynamics.
5. Refitting on all samples reproduces the sampling distribution (same mean, same std, up to noise): no update toward good actions; each iteration is fresh random shooting from the same distribution.
6. Evaluator first: cheapest to test offline on recorded rollouts, directly useful (failure detection/ranking checkpoints), no closed-loop risk. Experiment: on labelled rollouts (success/failure), score each episode by surprise or predicted progress; baseline = elapsed time (length null) and policy action variance; pre-register AUROC margin over the length null with episode bootstrap, paired. Only then try planning.
