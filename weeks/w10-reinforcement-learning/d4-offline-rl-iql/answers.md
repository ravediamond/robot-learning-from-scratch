# Model answers

1. The TD target needs max_a' Q(s', a') or Q(s', π(s')). Those actions may not be in the data, so their Q-values are unconstrained extrapolations. The max picks the largest errors, the bootstrap propagates them, and nothing corrects them because no new data arrives: extrapolation error, growing over-estimation.
2. V is regressed only on Q(s, a) of dataset actions (expectile instead of max). Q's target uses V(s'), never Q at a new action. The policy is weighted regression on dataset actions. No loss ever evaluates Q at an action outside the data.
3. How optimistic V is within the data's support. τ = 0.5: V is the mean of Q under the behavior policy (SARSA-like, evaluates the data-collection policy). τ → 1: V approaches the max over dataset actions in that state (more improvement, noisier, more sensitive to lucky outliers).
4. Offline RL wins when good behavior must be stitched from parts of different trajectories, when quality varies within episodes, and when rewards tell good from bad. If whole episodes are simply good or bad and labelled, filtering the top fraction and running BC is often equal and simpler: always run that baseline.
5. Teleop is the broad, high-quality prior: pretrain by imitation on it. The rollouts carry what teleop lacks: the policy's own failure states and recoveries. Use them by labelling success and progress (a value function), then either weight or condition the policy on advantage (RECAP-style, tomorrow), relabel interventions as corrections (DAgger-style), and train failure detectors. Keep sampling ratios explicit so 100 hours are not drowned by 10,000; evaluate on the real robot with paired starts.
6. The learned Q is exactly the object that may be wrong outside the data, and the policy was optimised to exploit it. Offline policy evaluation estimators are high-variance. So: validate value and policy choices on held-out returns only as a filter, then run real (or simulated) paired rollouts with confidence intervals, start with a conservative operating point, and add the new rollouts to the dataset.
