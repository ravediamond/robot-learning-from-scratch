# Model answers

1. y = r + γ(1 − done)(min(Q1', Q2')(s', a') − α log π(a'|s')), a' ~ π(·|s'). Bootstrap: learn from single transitions instead of full returns, which allows off-policy reuse. Twin minimum: Q-learning's max over noisy estimates over-estimates; the minimum of two critics is a cheap pessimistic correction. Entropy term: the agent is paid for staying random where it does not matter, which gives exploration and robustness.
2. π is a density over a, not over u. a = tanh(u) compresses large |u| into a thin band near ±1, so density there is p(u)/(1 − a²). Without the term, log π is too low near the bounds, the entropy looks higher than it is, the temperature and actor are tuned against a wrong quantity, and the actor drifts to saturated actions.
3. Function approximation + bootstrapping + off-policy data can diverge. SAC softens it with target networks (slow-moving bootstrap targets), the twin minimum (pessimism), and by keeping the policy stochastic and close to the data it generated. Offline, extra conservatism is needed (W10 D4).
4. Demonstrations in the buffer from the start (sampled 50/50 with online data), human interventions through a teleoperation device when the policy drifts (corrections become data), a learned success classifier as the sparse reward, a pretrained frozen vision encoder, a high update-to-data ratio with regularised critics, a low-level impedance controller for safe contact, and tasks with a small reset region.
5. Gradient updates per environment step. Real steps are expensive, so more updates per step extract more from each one. Too high: the critics over-fit the few early transitions and over-estimate; plasticity is lost. Mitigations: critic ensembles, layer norm or dropout in critics (REDQ, DroQ), periodic resets.
6. Over-estimation feeding itself through the bootstrap: the target grows with the estimate. Check done and time-limit handling, the reward scale, that targets carry no gradient and use the target networks. Fixes: smaller τ, twin minimum actually applied, lower learning rate, layer norm in the critic, clip or normalise rewards, lower UTD; offline, add conservatism (CQL or IQL).
