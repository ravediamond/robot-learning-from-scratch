# Questions (out loud)

1. Sparse success reward vs dense progress reward: what does each cost to obtain, and what does each do to RL?
2. Why choose a success-classifier threshold for precision and not accuracy? What does the agent do with a false positive?
3. Why are ordering pairs built inside one episode? When are cross-trajectory comparisons valid (Robometer)?
4. Your classifier is 98% accurate offline and RL "solves" the task in 10 minutes with a 0% real success rate. What happened, and how do you fix the loop?
5. Give three ways to get a reward signal from a VLM or video model, and the main weakness of each.
6. How would you validate a reward model before spending robot hours on RL with it?
