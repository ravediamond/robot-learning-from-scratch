# Questions (out loud)

1. Walk through the rollout of a new policy from "evaluation passed" to "whole fleet", with the gate at each stage.
2. What does shadow mode tell you and what can it not tell you?
3. Which field metrics do you monitor after a rollout, and which one triggers a rollback?
4. Why compare rates with a test rather than eyeballing them? What is the cost of each error?
5. How do you roll back on a robot that is in the middle of a task?
6. How do you keep 50 robots' software, firmware, calibration and models consistent, and know when they are not?
