1. Give your numbers: val MSE and success for both heads, the paired table, and the McNemar p from your D2 code. What do you ship?
2. Why does the MSE head win on val MSE? Is val MSE useless, then? What is it good for?
3. What else besides success would you log per episode, and why? (Think about what you needed in your register.)
4. The review bug: why did the ranking flip between runs? How many trials would hide this kind of noise, and is that the right fix?
5. Your SO-101 bowls evaluations: how did you fix start states on the real arm? What breaks pairing on hardware?
