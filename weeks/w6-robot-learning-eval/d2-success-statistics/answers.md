Pairing removes the variance due to start-state difficulty (hard layouts fail for both): only the policy difference remains, so the variance of the difference is smaller and fewer trials are needed. Breaks when starts are not truly reproduced (objects placed differently), when conditions drift between the A and B runs (lighting, battery, servo temperature): interleave A and B runs and reset carefully.
3. Concordant pairs (both succeed / both fail) carry no information about which policy is better; under the null, each discordant pair is equally likely to favour A or B: a binomial test on the discordant pairs is exact.
