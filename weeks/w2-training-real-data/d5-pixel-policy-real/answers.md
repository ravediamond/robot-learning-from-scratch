# Model answers

1. Expected: pixel < state-only < hold, but the gap is smaller than on the toy. On the bowls task the start layouts vary less than the dot world, and the state already says a lot (where the arm is in the motion); the image matters mostly for where the bowls are. At 64×64 the bowls are a few pixels; 15 epochs on 60 episodes is little. And MSE averages over all steps: the image matters most at a few decision points.
2. It removes the image's information while keeping everything else identical; if the error does not get worse, the image was not used. Comparing val losses between two differently-trained models mixes capacity, optimization noise and data; the blank-image test asks the trained model directly.
3. Val is computed with training-time augmentation, so it is neither deterministic nor the condition the robot sees. Val < train usually signals that train and val are measured differently (augmentation, dropout, train loss averaged over an epoch while weights improve). Rule: evaluate in the deployment condition, compare like with like.
4. Train once with both, evaluate on the same val episodes with (a) both cameras, (b) top blanked, (c) wrist blanked; paired per episode, bootstrap the difference. Better on the robot: same 20 start layouts, 3 conditions, success rate with Wilson CIs, pre-registered bar (e.g. blanking a camera drops success by ≥ 20 points = it relies on it). Your register's "wrist blank" row did this.
5. If decoding is, say, 300 frames/s and a training step consumes 64 frames in 20 ms (3,200 frames/s), on-the-fly decoding makes the GPU wait ~90% of the time. Decode once to resized frames, cache them, and load from memory/disk; or decode in many workers. LeRobot's video backend tuning (torchcodec, workers) exists for this reason.
6. (1) Calibration drift between data collection and now (check joint readings at a known pose: minutes). (2) Camera moved (compare a current frame with a training frame: minutes). (3) Action-state lag/latency at inference (log timestamps: minutes). (4) Normalization stats or checkpoint mismatch (reload checks: minutes). (5) Model precision limit: 64×64 cannot resolve 2 cm (check val error in cm for the place phase, then try higher resolution or the wrist camera: hours).
