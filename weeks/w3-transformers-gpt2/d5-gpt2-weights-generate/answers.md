# Model answers

1. (1) Count loaded tensors vs expected; any silently skipped key? (2) Transposes: a square weight (c_proj 768×768) loads untransposed without a shape error. (3) LayerNorm eps (1e-5 vs 1e-6), GELU variant (tanh vs exact). (4) Attention scale and mask (causal, diagonal). (5) Tying, position embeddings offset. Compare activations layer by layer with hooks to find the first one that differs.
2. Plausible text can come from a partially broken model (a wrong transpose in one layer still yields fluent-ish text). Exact logits on fixed inputs test every weight and every operation; the error localizes with per-layer comparison.
3. Per layer, K and V for every past token: 2 × L × T × D values = 2 × 12 × 1024 × 768 × 2 bytes ≈ 37.7 MB. Each new token computes its own q, k, v, appends k, v, and attends over the cache: O(T·D) per token instead of recomputing the whole prefix O(T²·D).
4. Temperature rescales logits (sharper < 1, flatter > 1); top-k keeps the k most likely tokens; top-p keeps the smallest set with cumulative probability ≥ p. For discrete action tokens at deployment: greedy or low temperature (repeatability, no rare bad actions), maybe top-k small; sampling diversity is for exploration or best-of-N, not for safe execution.
5. Typical: train ~1.2, val ~1.4–1.6: a gap from the small corpus and topic shift between chapters. The sample shows what the loss hides: whether it learned words, markdown structure, numbers, your phrases ("pre-registered bar") versus gibberish.
