# Follow-ups the interviewer asks once your code passes

m1: What is the memory of your implementation at T = 8k? How would you add a padding mask? Where would RoPE go?
m2: Why does 1 Euler step give the mean? How do you make it faster at inference? What changes for a diffusion (DDPM) head?
m3: Why sum the cost over the horizon? What happens with N = 20? How do you warm-start the next replanning step?
m4: Why paired? When is McNemar better than the bootstrap here? How many starts would you need to detect a 10-point gain?
