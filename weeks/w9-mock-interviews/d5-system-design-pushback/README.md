# W9 D5: System design, pushback, and the final retro

Primers: `knowledge/08-question-bank.md` (sections C and E). Book: 16 (the interview), and
`reference/journal/book/exercises/interview/` (opener, pushback drills, fault cards).

No tests today. Three recorded rounds, then a retro. Use a timer and a whiteboard (paper is fine).

## Round 1: system design (35 min): `system_design.md`
"Design the data flywheel for a fleet of 10 low-cost arms doing a household tidying task at customer
sites." Fill the template, then present it in 10 minutes, recorded.

## Round 2: pushback (20 min): `pushback.md`
Five interviewer statements. For each: 60 seconds, out loud. Hold your ground with evidence, or update
with a reason. Never just agree.

## Round 3: your opener (5 min)
Use `reference/journal/book/exercises/interview/opener.md`. Record it twice; keep the better one.

## Retro: `final_retro.md`
Re-run three earlier mocks you did badly on (from PROGRESS.md). Write the retro.
