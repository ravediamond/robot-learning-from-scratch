# Questions (out loud)

1. Why do multiple comparisons inflate false positives? Holm vs Bonferroni vs controlling the false discovery rate: when each?
2. What do rank probabilities tell a reader that a table of success rates with CIs does not?
3. Compute, roughly, the minimum detectable difference for 50 rollouts per policy at 50% success. What do you do with that number?
4. When does pairing reduce the number of rollouts you need, and when does it not help?
5. You peek at results every 10 rollouts and stop when p < 0.05. What goes wrong, and what are two correct ways to stop early?
6. A reviewer asks for 5 seeds of training per policy. How does that change the analysis?
