# Model answers

1. Watch 20–30 failures of the baseline with someone who knows the task, write candidate causes, merge until each is defined by observable evidence ("gripper closes and the object is not between the fingers when lift starts"), add "other" with a free-text note, and freeze the list before the comparison. Usually 5–8 categories: enough to separate what you would fix differently, few enough to label reliably.
2. Agreement expected by chance from each rater's label frequencies: two raters who both say "grasp" 90% of the time agree 82% by chance. κ above about 0.6 (substantial) is a usable bar for per-cause rates; below that, merge categories or sharpen definitions and relabel.
3. Primary tag (the first thing that went irreversibly wrong) for rates: categories sum to the failure rate and comparisons are clean. Optionally secondary tags for analysis. Multi-label alone makes rates hard to read and inflates causes that co-occur with others.
4. The funnel is objective and automatic (stages can be detected from state or simple checks) and says where in the task a policy stops; the taxonomy says why, including failures that do not map to a stage (collisions, timeouts, safety stops). Together: "it stops at grasp, and the grasp failures are perception (wrong position) rather than gripper timing".
5. Check the starts: 3 and 7 are near the edge of the camera view or in a reflection. Collect 20–30 targeted demonstrations around those configurations (and their neighbours), keep the rest of the mixture, retrain, and re-evaluate on the same start list, pre-registering "grasp failures on starts 3 and 7 drop from 9/10 to under 3/10 without losing elsewhere". Also check whether it is a perception problem (lighting) that data will not fix.
6. Derive stage events from proprioception and gripper state (gripper closed with no load = grasp miss; object height for lift), add a learned classifier on video for the rest, and treat it as a measurement instrument: validate against human labels on a held-out set (per-class precision/recall, κ against humans), keep a human audit sample every session, and report the automatic tags with that measured agreement.
