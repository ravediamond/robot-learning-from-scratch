# Questions (out loud)

1. Describe the data flywheel of a deployed robot policy, step by step, with the human cost of each step.
2. Why are interventions more valuable per minute than fresh demonstrations? What are their biases?
3. How do you choose which autonomous episodes humans review?
4. You have budget for 200 new demos next week. How do you decide where they go?
5. How do you prove the flywheel works, rather than just running it?
6. Open problem (Skild/PI-style): "10,000 hours of teleop and 100 hours of autonomous rollouts with failures. What do you do with them?"
