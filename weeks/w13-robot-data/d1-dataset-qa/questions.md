# Questions (out loud)

1. List the checks you would run on every new teleoperation upload, and what each one protects the policy from.
2. Why median and MAD instead of mean and standard deviation for outliers? What is masking?
3. Quarantine or delete? Who decides, and how is the decision recorded?
4. How do you detect a calibration change in the middle of a dataset?
5. A check flags 30% of one station's episodes. What do you do in the next hour, and in the next week?
6. How would you know your checks are good enough? What is their false-positive cost?
