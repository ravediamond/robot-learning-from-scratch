# Questions (out loud)

1. How do you choose mixture weights when one source is 20× larger than yours? What does the temperature trade?
2. What goes wrong when a small source is sampled at a high weight? How do you detect it?
3. How would you measure the value of one more hour of teleoperation of a given task?
4. Your ablation shows −4 points when removing a source. What else must you report before anyone believes it?
5. Cross-embodiment data (other robots): when does it help a SO-101 policy, and how would you test it?
6. Sim data in the mixture: what can it teach, and how do you stop it from teaching the sim's physics?
