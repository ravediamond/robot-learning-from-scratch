2. Why does the reparameterization trick allow backpropagation through sampling? What would you do without it?
3. β in the VAE: what happens with β → 0? With β very large (predict item 5)? Which do you want for a policy?

5. The review bug: what is the actual std when logvar = −4? When logvar = 4? Why does it "never settle"?
