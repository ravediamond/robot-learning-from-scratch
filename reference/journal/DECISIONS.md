## Standing rules (owner)

From [`HANDOFF.md`](../projects/vla-jepa-world-model/HANDOFF.md) and the protocols:
- No Claude attribution in commits, PRs or comments.
- The owner opens issues and PRs on external repos.
- Robot: nothing moves without the owner's "go"; "stop" kills the run; outcome labels are always the owner's.
- Costs: as low as possible; whole-job cost; $15 per day cap across all work; ask before any full training run;
  destroy instances when results are fetched; no write tokens on the robot machine or on rented instances.
- Data: small files in git; videos and checkpoints never in git.
- Experiments: protocol and pass rule frozen before the number; anything decided after is labelled exploratory.
- Never train the policy on raw failures; failures feed the world model, the cost and the judge.
