   `own`: s_succ 0.825 [0.70, 0.93]; `pol_rate` (policy identity alone) **0.87** [0.77, 0.95]; on the 50 non-TurboVLA episodes
   s_succ is **0.78**. The 69 include 17 TurboVLA failures (REAP row, 2026-10-23), so a score that reads "which policy" gets 0.87
   for free. Abstract ("TOPReward reaches 0.82") and §4 must say: 0.82 on 69, *below* policy identity (0.87), 0.78 once
   TurboVLA is removed — i.e. not shown to beat the identity null, same as Robometer on the same set (0.577 vs identity 0.878
   in G9a-own). The two-regime sentence survives on Flora / Qwen3.5-27B (at rest, 50 episodes), not on TOPReward.
2. **Length on the same 69 is 0.56, not 0.52.** §4 and the abstract compare TOPReward on 69 to length on 88 (0.52).
   `topreward.json` `own.neg_len` = 0.560 [0.42, 0.69]. Use the paired number on the same episodes.
3. **Flora's threshold k = 4 was chosen in-sample** on the same 50 episodes it is scored on (verifier card, EXPERIMENTS
   2026-10-23: "k in-sample"); the same for Qwen3.5-27B and Gemini. §5 and §6 must say so; the 8/8 at 2/42 is an in-sample
   operating point, the AUROC 1.00 is the threshold-free number to lead with.
4. **Fig. 3 mixes two tasks.** Flora-27B and Qwen3.5-27B are *judges at rest* (one score per finished episode); the other
   five points are *mid-episode monitors* (a score per window, bar = caught before onset at ≤ 1/20 false interventions).
   Putting both in one "bar" box says a rest judge "meets the monitor bar", which it was never asked to. Split the figure
   (two panels) or mark the two judges with a different glyph and say in the caption that their bar is G-flora's, not the
   monitor bar.
