## Must fix

1. **The title overstates the split.** "Knows what happened, not what will": the rest readout (0.95) is "what happened",
   but the grasp-anchor readout (0.82) is taken **15 frames before the first gripper close** — the grasp has not
   happened yet. That is a short-horizon forecast *from the current latent*. What fails is not forecasting; it is the
   **predictor's imagination adding nothing** (imagined 0.70 < current 0.82) and every use that needs a multi-step
   rollout. Reword: "the encoder reads the outcome, the predictor adds nothing" (title candidate: *A small JEPA world
   model: the encoder knows, the predictor does not add*). §1's "does not turn that into a forecast" → "does not improve
   on it by imagining forward". Abstract sentence "It does not know what will happen" → "Imagining forward does not add
   to what the current latent already reads".
2. **G10's 1.000 was scored against the judge's labels** (gaps/README: "G10's 1.000 was scored against judge labels on
   87 rollouts"). §4 says "on a larger rollout set"; it must say "against the G9c judge's labels, not the operator's",
   or a reader takes 1.000 as a stronger result than the 0.95 here.
3. **Counts: 13 + 6 = 19, the register has 20.** §1: "13 bars committed before their result, 6 in the same commit" — the
   twentieth (no bar, or not resolvable) must be named, and the verdict tally (3 + 1 + 2 + 14 = 20) reconciled with it.
4. **"Neither mainly kinematics nor the better pose reader"** (§3) is an inference the numbers do not carry: R² 0.565
   means more than half of the linear joint variance is in the latent. Say what is measured: "the encoder holds about
   half of the joint variance linearly (R² 0.565; the frozen DINOv2-S 0.867)", and let §4 make the point that it holds
   outcome information the pose readers do not.
